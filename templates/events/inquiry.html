3 Competitive Events

Cryptanalysis, on-the-spot experiments, and pure written communication.

Codebusters

TEAM OF 3

A timed cryptanalysis exam — decode encrypted messages using both historical and modern ciphers.

What You'll Learn

Solving monoalphabetic ciphers: Aristocrats, Patristocrats, Xenocrypts
Cryptanalysis of Vigenère, Hill, and other polyalphabetic ciphers
Letter-frequency analysis and pattern recognition
Splitting problems across a team of three to work efficiently under time pressure

Experimental Design

TEAM OF 3

Given unfamiliar materials, teams design, run, and write up an original experiment entirely on the spot.

What You'll Learn

Writing a testable problem statement and hypothesis
Identifying independent, dependent, and controlled variables
Collecting and graphing real data within a roughly 50-minute window
Analyzing results and reporting conclusions in a structured write-up

Write It Do It

TEAM OF 2

One teammate writes instructions for building a structure; the other builds it from those instructions alone, with no drawings allowed.

What You'll Learn

Writing clear, unambiguous step-by-step instructions
Describing a 3D structure using only text
Building an accurate replica from a partner's written directions
Scored on how closely the final build matches the original
